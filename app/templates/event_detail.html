🎫 Check-In Process

  • 1. After you register, you'll get a digital ticket with a unique QR code. Save the link or print it.
  • 2. On event day, head to the check-in desk at {{ event.venue }} and show your QR code.
  • 3. A volunteer scans it once, and you're in. The whole thing takes a few seconds.
  • ℹ️ Each ticket works exactly once — if it's been scanned already, re-entry won't be accepted.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a registration fee?
No — this event is free. You'll receive a digital pass at no cost.
Can I register more than once with the same email?
No. Each email can only be used for one registration per event. If you need to update your details, contact the organizers.
I lost my ticket link. How do I get it back?
Use the "Retrieve your ticket" option on the homepage. You'll need your registered email and Ticket Code.
Can someone else check in with my ticket?
Tickets are non-transferable. The name on the ticket should match the attendee.

🛟 Need Help?

If you run into any issues with registration or your ticket, reach out to the event organizers directly. On event day, volunteers at the check-in desk can help with manual verification if your QR code isn't scanning.
